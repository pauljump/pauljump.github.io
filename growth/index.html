01 / thesis

A task.
A receipt.
A teammate.

The smallest durable unit of Claude growth is not a message. It is a finished thing that earns a second user. That artifact should show what happened, what is supported, what is still uncertain, and what the next person can do.

01 / activationDo the job.

Get a user to a meaningful outcome before asking them to learn a system.

02 / expansionShow the work.

Make the result easy to hand off without leaking context or hiding uncertainty.

03 / retentionReturn to it.

Let the next task begin where the last one ended, with a reason to come back.

02 / play the loop

What growth feels like when the product does the selling.

Choose the starting context. Then move one job through the loop. This is a product hypothesis, not a claim about current Claude behavior.

03 / the growth system

One loop.
Three surfaces.

I would organize the work around the product’s actual expansion path, not a generic funnel. The metric changes as the artifact travels.

loop 01

Person → outcome

Make the first session feel like a completed job. Onboarding should adapt to the work, not make the user classify themselves before they get value.

north star: first_useful_outcome
loop 02

Outcome → team

Turn a finished result into a safe, scoped handoff. The second user should understand why this matters before they understand how Claude works.

north star: second_user_continues
loop 03

Team → system

Let repeated team behavior graduate into a connected workflow with the right permissions, controls, and plan value.

north star: repeated_workflow_expands

04 / first experiments

Small bets with a long memory.

The first month should produce learning that compounds. Each experiment has a clear user promise, a kill condition, and an instrumentation path.

WEEK 01Instrument the actual win

Define the event that means a user got useful work back. Segment by task, surface, plan, and new versus returning user.

WEEK 02Improve the first job

Test role-native entry points against the blank chat. Optimize for completed work, not clicks or time in onboarding.

WEEK 03Ship the handoff

Let a user share a scoped artifact with a next action. Measure whether a second person continues the work.

WEEK 04Scale or kill

Read the quality and safety signals with the growth numbers. Keep only loops that improve value without eroding trust.

06 / the non-negotiable

No dark patterns.

I would not optimize Claude growth by manufacturing anxiety, hiding uncertainty, or turning private work into public content by default. The durable advantage is calibrated trust: users know what Claude did, what it did not do, and why the next step is worth taking.

That is the growth system I want to build: fast enough for a frontier lab, legible enough for a safety-minded one, and useful enough that the product earns its next user.
