01 / work

live work + demos

forty things i built, each explained in one plain-language paragraph.

Kit Home

live ↗

a parent companion that turns authorized school, clinical, and insurance records into one sourced timeline, plus briefs and drafts for each audience. records from institutions that do not share context finally sit together. the typed source record matters more than the chat box.

Kit Studio

live ↗

a workflow for designing and shipping one-off ipad apps for individual kids, including gift, reimbursable, and provider-led projects. the app starts with one kid instead of a market segment. a tight concept review keeps the factory from building the wrong app quickly.

Colosseum

live demo ↗

a live oxford-format debate app. several ai models argue from assigned positions, cite sources, face a separate judge, and measure how the audience vote moved. you can inspect disagreement instead of accepting one finished answer. the judge and source trail are harder than generating the arguments.

unignorable

live demo ↗

a map of 455,000 nyc 311 reports about encampments, drug activity, and homeless assistance, including repeat reports and reopen rates. the city's own records show the same places coming back. closed tickets are not the same thing as resolved problems.

Pricefixed

live ↗

open tools that join nyc apartment listings, public records, unit price history, and landlord portfolios in one local database. rent history and ownership become visible at the unit level. resolving addresses across messy datasets is most of the product.

vibecheck

live demo ↗

a local tool that reads claude code, codex, and cursor sessions and produces a report on how someone builds with ai. session data stays on the machine. the evidence is work someone already did, not another personality quiz. a useful profile needs quotes and receipts from the underlying sessions.

Best Day

live ↗

one tap returns a verified, door-to-door outing for the current place and time. separate surfaces cover family days, dates, and places to work. it removes both the form and the list of options. verification is the expensive part, not generating the itinerary.

Slopify

live ↗

a public listening and sharing site for generated music, paired with an ios app that creates and stores audio locally. the web handles listening while the phone handles private creation. local generation needs provenance, storage, and publishing before it needs a bigger model.

Unhinged

live demo ↗

an iphone and apple watch app that creates a private date report from 16 on-device audio and biometric signals. the whole report can be made without uploading raw audio. the baseline and weighting matter more than any single signal.

StuyWatch

live demo ↗

an apartment tracker for stuytown, pcv, kips bay, parker towers, and 8 spruce. it stores hourly listings, price changes, and market history. a renter can see changes that disappear from the listing page. history becomes useful after the scraper survives long enough to collect it.

Comms Engine

live ↗

an intelligence system for public affairs work that joins public data, private client records, and tracked outcomes through reusable domain lenses. the same system can be pointed at a new policy domain without rebuilding the data model. the joins and outcome history matter more than the chat surface.

Permit Atlas

live ↗

a cited agent that researches a us city's permit path, plus a ledger of post-permit events across 22 jurisdictions. every field links back to an official source. permission does not stop being a workflow after the permit is issued.

GraphCo

offline

a local ai setup for small law firms, with practice-area simulators and deployment to the firm's own mac mini and model accounts. a firm can inspect the workflow before connecting its own matters. adoption starts with one real practice task, not a general assistant.

Curtain Call

live ↗

a directory of broadway lotteries with direct links to each show's official entry page. one page replaces a set of fragmented ticket sites. an aggregator is only useful when the source links stay current.

The Filing

live ↗

a job research tool that matches a greenhouse or lever role with the employer's h-1b filing history and a set of analyst views. the immigration data is attached to the job someone is already considering. company history needs to be narrowed to the actual role before it helps.

Gateclock

live demo ↗

a dashboard that polls free jfk and sfo security-wait sources and stores checkpoint readings as a time series. several incomplete feeds become one usable view. source reliability changes by airport, checkpoint, and time of day.

Inflation

live ↗

a personal price index built from past instacart orders, with unit prices by item, store, and date. it answers price questions using a person's own receipts. normalizing package sizes is harder than importing the orders.

Vacay

live ↗

a hotel-rate monitor that checks room and date combinations every four hours and records price and availability changes. it watches the exact trip instead of showing another search result. booking sites require browser-level collection and careful room matching.

FSD vs Air

offline

a trip comparison that combines tesla drive data with flight schedules and airport time. it answers drive or fly with personal door-to-door data. airport time changes the result more often than flight time does.

Punchcard

offline

a loyalty and referral app for pest-control customers, built around repeat service visits. it adds a customer loop without replacing the field-service system. vertical software starts with one existing behavior.

Sasquatch

live ↗

a cooperative family activity game that uses names, surroundings, energy level, and inside or outside context to generate missions for kids and parents. the phone stays with the parent while the game happens in the room. context-aware missions and shared goals work better than scores or competition.

Slash Usage

live ↗

a private dashboard for cli model availability, observed usage, reset windows, and api credits across eight providers. one screen replaces a pile of provider pages and cli commands. when a provider does not expose quota, unavailable is better than a fake percentage.

So Annoying

live ↗

a source-audited catalog of recurring annoyances, kept separate as observations, reviewed problems, solution proposals, and validation results. the source trail stays attached while rough complaints become testable problems. frequency, importance, and ai-solvability are different judgments.

Build a Game

live ↗

a workshop where a kid answers four questions and a parent uses an ai coding agent to ship the requested game into a tablet vault. thirteen games are live. a kid's words become playable software the same day. short build loops give kids enough context to direct the next version.

Zapper Boy vs. Lava Man

live ↗

a tap-to-play superhero story game for kids, published as a static web app. a small story can work as a game without an app store or account. one clear interaction is enough for a young kid to keep moving through a story.

First Date

live demo ↗

a two-person date app. each person answers seven questions privately, then the system creates the date and keeps the result sealed until both finish. two private inputs become one shared experience. sequence and consent matter more than the generated prose.

Junto

live demo ↗

a school-specific public ledger of projects students built with ai, with branded and access-controlled versions for each institution. the argument for ai is the work students can open and inspect. a project record is more useful than an abstract ai curriculum.

ORIGIN

live demo ↗

a reflex game that teaches the path from a working directory to a local commit and a remote git origin. the git mental model becomes a physical sequence. repetition teaches the workflow faster than another diagram.

Sprezzatura

live demo ↗

an nyc outing planner that turns a night and a few constraints into a complete sequence of places. it produces the plan instead of another list of recommendations. distance and operating hours break more plans than the model does.

Walkie

live demo ↗

a voice interface for directing a team of coding agents during a walk, including task assignment, progress, and pull requests. the management surface works without a desk. voice is good for intent, but review still needs a visual artifact.

BARkey

demo ↗

an on-device keyboard that learns from roughly 500 messages and exports a portable writing-style prompt. the useful output survives after the app is deleted. a personal model should have a clear export and a clear end state.

BooWho

demo ↗

an apple watch video player for diaper changes, with small video packs synced from the phone. the distraction sits on the parent's wrist instead of in the kid's hands. a narrow moment can justify its own watch app.

Chomp

demo ↗

a food logger that turns a spoken meal into calories, protein, and fiber, with apple health and apple watch support. logging takes one sentence instead of a database search. speed matters more than perfect nutrition estimates for daily use.

Jump Bank

demo ↗

a native kids' bank with account opening, a computed ledger, voice wishes, savings tiers, and a reason attached to each transaction. the account feels real while the money remains a family ledger. kids understand transactions better when every one has a story.

Neverlist

demo ↗

an off-market real-estate tool that finds buildings with long gaps between listings and assembles owner contact records. it searches for the inventory that does not appear on listing sites. absence becomes a signal only after ownership and transaction history are joined.

PaperClaw

demo ↗

an imessage paper-trading app with fake money, live prices, stock and options calls, prediction-market bets, and group receipts. a claim in the group chat becomes a timestamped position. the receipt and settlement loop matter more than a brokerage-style interface.

RexAir

demo ↗

a flight app built for my son. it searches real routes and dates, books a seat in the app, tracks the aircraft, and keeps a flight history. his interest in flights becomes the interface for learning routes, time, and aircraft. software for one person can use real data without becoming a general product.

Sightline

demo ↗

a native ipad app that teaches standard music notation through see, find, play, and hear exercises. it uses my son's absolute pitch as the answer key without making it the exercise. the existing strength should support the new skill, not replace it.

The Move

demo ↗

a 28-day private decision process for two partners, with three questions a day and a shared reveal at the end. each person can answer without anchoring on the other person's response. good decision software controls when information is revealed.

Walt's Workshop

demo ↗

an ipad vault where my son records what he wants built and receives new games made from his requests. thirteen games have shipped. he directs the work in his own words and sees the result appear in his app. asking, describing, choosing, and checking are enough structure for a kid to direct an agent.
