01

field work → product rail

Kit · New York OPWDD Self-Direction

Selected product cases

Turn a paper-and-phone workflow into an approval-readiness system.

This began as a passion project with a nonprofit I met through my board work with the Simons Foundation. In New York OPWDD Self-Direction, a family can have an approved budget and still spend weeks moving one request through a provider, care manager, support broker, and Fiscal Intermediary. The work was paper, email, phone calls, and one-off packets. I built the system that compiles that work before a family has to carry it.

What the field looked like

The request lived between institutions

A parent found a class or service, then became the integration layer. They had to determine the funding route, establish the actual offering and provider facts, get the right evidence, and learn the FI’s submission rules. Most of that work was repeated the next time another family asked for the same service.

The product decision

Compile the route before the request arrives

Kit turns policy, FI process, provider evidence, and the family’s private facts into a bounded readiness prediction: likely fundable, fundable with fixes, unlikely as described, or insufficient verified information. It produces the packet and next action behind that result, rather than a black-box score.

What the system actually is

Shared evidence stays reusable. Private case data and final decisions do not.

01 Statewide rail

One versioned decision and evidence contract per service branch determines the required packet and the route it belongs on.

02 FI adapter

The selected FI adds its exact form, channel, timing, pre-approval, vendor setup, and correction workflow without forking statewide policy.

03 Provider evidence graph

Organization, location, offering, price, payee, and reimbursement facts are source-linked, hashed, and reusable across family requests instead of rediscovered ad hoc.

04 Family case record

The product joins the shared record to the Life Plan, budget, and family evidence, then drafts the smallest complete packet for human review.

05 Outcome ledger

Submission, deficiency, revision, decision, and payment are recorded against the exact route, FI adapter, and evidence state.

The live product at opwddkit.com is the public surface of that system, not a generic provider list. A deterministic miner resolves a provider to its actual location, offering, and reimbursement path before that evidence is reusable in a family packet.

1,275 provider and program records ingested; 1,263 are currently published in the live, source-backed catalog
127 FI-scoped, quoted process claims from 51 reviewed documents across 25 Fiscal Intermediaries
122 executable statewide routing and evidence contracts across 85 expense and service families; 120 are atomically bound

How it gets better

Every FI has an adapter slot. When a published rule is captured it fills the exact form, timing, or payment step; when it is missing, Kit creates a confirmation job instead of copying another FI’s process. That makes coverage additive. With explicit consent, every returned packet, deficiency, revision, decision, and payment also becomes a calibration record for the exact route, FI adapter, and family fact pattern. Sparse or conflicting cohorts abstain; a learned pattern never silently rewrites a deterministic rule.

02

field signal → reliability layer

Local legal AI · Harvey LAB

A legal AI pilot is not the product. The transferable method is.

A small law firm had banned cloud AI because its client material could not leave the office. Working with Harvey LAB’s public corpus, I inventoried its 25 practice areas, 1,196 workflow families, 1,749 task scenarios, and 14,061 source documents. The goal was never to prove that one local model could compare two documents. It was to build a provider-neutral method that can carry into any legal area: define the task and accepted output, bind it to the right sources, measure retrieval and omissions separately, then require the system to cite, abstain, or escalate.

What we did not build

A better one-off legal demo

A fluent answer can miss an issue, cite the wrong provision, or turn uncertainty into confidence. Making that answer easier to produce would only make a serious error harder to see. The useful question was what has to be true before any legal AI result enters a real workflow.

The product decision

Make the methodology portable, not the prompt

Between practice areas, the task contract, source set, and acceptance criteria change. The underlying loop does not: retrieval baseline, reranking, evidence selection, missing-issue checks, an issue ledger, and a locked answer before scoring. Every run pins the task, corpus, model, evidence, and score so the next miss becomes a benchmark case instead of product lore.

03

constraint → technical judgment

Itchy · OpenAI Parameter Golf

Build the benchmark that lets you abandon the obvious architecture.

Parameter Golf asked for the strongest model in a 16 MB artifact, but the question I cared about was larger: could a capable model run locally beside the data that should not leave a machine, like legal files or medical records? More than 600 submissions were optimizing inside the same BPE-transformer neighborhood. I started with a different premise: raw bytes, no tokenizer, and a model built small from birth. Then I wrote the matched benchmark that would let the new approach lose honestly before anyone optimized around it.

The nontraditional bet

Build small from birth

The existing leaders had already taught the field how to shave points off the familiar architecture. I built the model, data pipeline, training loop, quantization path, local MLX implementation, CUDA path, and comparison harness around a 256-byte vocabulary. No tokenizer or large embedding table meant more of the 16 MB budget could go to the network itself.

The product-quality gate

Invalidate the exciting result

A new architecture is only useful if it has a fair target to optimize against. I held the data, loss, hardware, sequence regime, and artifact budget constant. When an early patching run looked exceptional, I traced the data flow and found that 11 of every 12 target bytes were visible in the input. I invalidated the result, removed the leakage, and kept the clean rerun beside the original instead of optimizing around a false win.

The clean comparison made the next decision clear: local, confidential workloads need a better systems path before they need a victory lap. The leak stayed in the record too, because the benchmark was the product work, not a step before it.

04

family need → software factory

Kit Studio · Special interests and private games

Build the game the kid actually asked for.

Through my board work with the Simons Foundation, I kept hearing a small version of a hard problem from parents: there are no games for my kid. I started building private, parent-reviewed games around a child’s real interests, language, and sensory needs. It has been the most rewarding work I have done. The first vault holds 13 real games. What compounds is the software factory behind them.

The input is not a diagnosis

Start with the thing that already holds attention

A parent may not know which app their child needs. They do know what they return to, how they describe it, what overwhelms them, and what rules make play feel safe. That becomes a private profile and a build brief, not a generic personalization form.

The product decision

Build an interest-to-game production system

The shared layer resolves a child’s special interest into reusable patterns and game mechanics. The private layer adds their vocabulary, accessibility constraints, and parent rules. The factory then creates one self-contained game for parent review and a private vault, instead of turning children’s data into a public content feed.

The system

01Ask

A parent requests a free game in plain language, including the rules that matter.

02Resolve

The request maps to an interest record and the mechanics, sensory defaults, and language it suggests.

03Build

A constrained builder creates a touch-first game with no accounts, ads, fail states, or surprise stimulation.

04Approve

The parent sees it before the child does. The game belongs in that family’s private vault.

05

evidence → synthetic role → regression loop

Teek · Simulation and evaluation layer

A synthetic user is only useful if it can disagree with you.

I needed a way to test the games before asking families to absorb every bad interaction. I pulled together public behavioral and design research, but refused to flatten it into a generic “kid persona” or a clinical score. Teek turns cited evidence into synthetic, constraint-based test roles: a declared situation, task, limits, expected behavior, and the result that would prove the simulation wrong.

The failure mode

Generic QA cannot see the bad experience.

A click-through can prove that a game launches. It cannot surface whether a sudden state change, reading-heavy prompt, unclear goal, or losing mechanic breaks the experience for the person the game was meant to reach. A plausible-sounding persona does not solve that either. It just turns a hunch into a longer prompt.

The product decision

Model the conditions, never a particular child.

Teek keeps the evidence separate from the test role. The role can specify an observable constraint such as touch-first interaction, low tolerance for surprise, an interest-specific entry point, or recovery after an error. It cannot diagnose, infer a child’s inner state, or stand in for real user research. Parent rules remain private inputs, not training data.

The simulation stack

Evidence determines the role. The role determines the test. The test can fail.

01 Bound corpus

Public research, design material, and authored sources are dated, typed, and preserved with provenance.

02 Evidence ledger

Each usable claim carries its exact span, status, confidence, and expiry. Unsupported claims do not compile.

03 Constraint compiler

Claims become task conditions: modality, cognitive and sensory load, interest specificity, error recovery, and agency.

04 Scenario runner

A run pins the role, product build, initial state, task, and perturbation so the path is reproducible.

05 Counterexample loop

Telemetry, parent feedback, or held-out outcomes can invalidate the role. A miss becomes a regression case.

Where the layer gets used

Kit Studio Test the interaction before the family does.

Roles exercise first-use, surprise, failure, and recovery paths against the game’s parent-defined constraints. The output is a concrete product fix or an explicit question for a real family, never a claim that a child will behave a certain way.

Local legal AI Turn “would a firm trust this?” into a testable workflow.

A synthetic firm card pins the local-data posture, matter shape, source standard, review chain, and escalation threshold. The system then tests whether an answer arrives with the right evidence, refuses when it should, and routes uncertainty to human review.

Every evidence system Make field intuition executable.

The shared pattern is source, constraint, scenario, outcome. Kit uses it for approval-readiness paths; the miners use it to separate a loud anecdote from recurrence; Teek turns the same discipline into a reusable evaluation harness.

PriceFixed · NYC housing registry

Transparency is the product. The unit record is the infrastructure.

PriceFixed is a hobby project with a serious aim: make housing less opaque. Renters make high-stakes decisions with the weakest data in the market, while landlord-side pricing tools and fragmented records keep unit history, ownership, listing changes, and rent context hard to see. I started building a durable, public record for the unit itself. The registry work matters because every transparency product otherwise has to rebuild the same evidence from scratch.

The system

Public record and listing capture → deterministic building and unit resolution → evidence check → versioned registry → explicit coverage and gap queue.
