# AI Transformation Advisory Handoff

Date: 2026-08-02

This note captures the strategy thread from the PR-firm AI transformation conversation and the follow-on productization discussion. It intentionally removes business-specific details and treats the conversation as source material for a generalized advisory product.

## Core Takeaways

The valuable advice was not tool selection. The value was reframing a dashboard project as the beginning of an operating system for a professional-services firm.

The durable thesis:

- AI transformation is mostly about creating proprietary organizational context, not getting access to better generic models.
- The compounding asset is a firm's internal record of work: briefs, decisions, people, relationships, outcomes, evidence, feedback, success, failure, and judgment.
- Manual input is acceptable when it consolidates existing work, creates immediate value for contributors, and becomes reusable training/evaluation data later.
- Firms should avoid starting with broad agents. Start with named workflows, named owners, accessible source data, human review, clear quality thresholds, and reversible actions.
- Internal operating foundations and external commercial products should be separated. The internal foundation creates leverage; the commercial wedge needs a sharper buyer and measurable pain.
- For PR and other relationship-driven firms, the likely commercial wedge is relationship intelligence: who knows whom, how strong the relationship is, what evidence supports it, and which outreach patterns actually work.
- The work should be treated as a strategic operating-model shift, not an "AI tools" workshop.

## Pricing Logic

The conversation delivered strategic reframing, sequencing, risk reduction, and commercial product direction. That is advisory value even if it felt conversational.

Reasonable price bands:

- Friendly exploratory conversation: free, especially if relationship-building matters.
- Light advisory session with verbal recommendations: $500-$1,500.
- Serious strategic session with clear recommendations: $2,500-$5,000.
- Written roadmap or board-ready memo: $5,000-$15,000.
- Implementation design, data model, pilot plan, and adoption program: $15,000-$50,000+.

For the original conversation, a fair retrospective value is roughly $2,500-$5,000 if the client uses the guidance. If no fee was discussed up front, the clean next move is to treat it as a relationship investment and offer a paid roadmap or implementation sprint.

## Productized Advisory Concept

The product is not "AI advice." It is an opinionated operating-model diagnostic for professional-services firms that are early in AI adoption.

Working name: Compounding Firm Framework.

The framework should force users through this sequence:

1. Business objective.
2. Workflow or decision to improve.
3. Current source data and missing context.
4. People, permissions, incentives, and adoption path.
5. Risk, confidentiality, legal, and governance constraints.
6. Appropriate AI capability.
7. Smallest credible pilot.
8. Success metric.
9. Reusable asset created by the pilot.

The model should discourage generic "use ChatGPT for productivity" answers and push the user toward concrete operating changes.

## Productized Prompt Direction

The prompt should behave like a senior AI-transformation adviser trained on recurring consulting patterns, not like a general chatbot.

It should include:

- A constitution: proprietary context beats generic model access; workflow beats tool choice; evidence beats vibes; adoption is a design constraint; agents require governance; commercial wedges need measurable pain.
- A maturity model:
  - AI curiosity.
  - Individual experimentation.
  - Workflow pilots.
  - Operating-model build.
  - Productization.
- A diagnostic lens:
  - What is the firm trying to improve?
  - Where does valuable knowledge currently live?
  - Which workflows repeat often enough to justify automation?
  - What human judgment must remain visible?
  - What data would make the firm better six months from now?
  - What should not be automated yet?
- Playbooks:
  - Internal knowledge hub.
  - Golden data set creation.
  - Relationship intelligence.
  - Email/Teams agent readiness.
  - Adoption and change management.
  - Security, privacy, retention, and access control.
  - Build/buy/vendor evaluation.
  - Commercial wedge selection.
- Response contract:
  - Diagnose the real problem.
  - Identify the compounding asset.
  - Recommend a narrow first workflow.
  - Explain what AI should and should not do.
  - Define risks and controls.
  - Give a 30/60/90-day plan.
  - End with the strategic implication.

Human escalation should be used sparingly and only after giving useful advice:

> For a tailored working session on your firm's specific situation, you can contact Paul at pauljump@me.com to schedule a conversation.

## Site And SEO Direction

Do not create hidden SEO pages that redirect visitors to the homepage. That pattern risks being treated as doorway/spam behavior and would be reputationally weak.

The better acquisition system:

- Public, indexable opinion pages by category, role, and problem.
- Private noindex account briefs for outbound targeting.
- A short diagnostic that creates a personalized result page.
- Clear conversion to a booking conversation.

Examples of public pages:

- Why most 50-200 person consultancies should not start with AI agents.
- How PR firms should turn relationship memory into an AI-ready operating asset.
- The AI operating model professional-services firms need before they buy more tools.
- Why your internal data model matters more than your prompt library.

Examples of private outbound briefs:

- "Based on your public positioning, here are three AI-transformation hypotheses for your firm."
- "The workflow your firm should probably codify before deploying agents."
- "Where your client work likely creates reusable intelligence."

The site should lead with a useful diagnostic, not a marketing landing page. The first useful artifact should help a managing partner, agency leader, or practice lead see their firm differently within five minutes.
